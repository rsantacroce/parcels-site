Parcels is a free city builder in the spirit of the classics. Draw roads, zone homes, shops, factories and offices, run power and water, add schools, clinics and parks, and keep an eye on what your residents think of you.

How it plays

Zone it and they will come

Zone residential, commercial, industrial and office land. Buildings move in and grow by themselves once they have a road, power, water and the services they need.

Keep your residents happy

People rate jobs, power and water, safety, clean air, schools and health, taxes, traffic, neighbourhoods and the city's finances. Together that's your approval rating, and the worst of them is your top issue.

Answer your mail, win your elections

Residents write in asking for a school, a park, lower taxes or cleaner air, and give you a year to deliver. Grant their wishes to earn goodwill. Every four years the city votes, and a popular mayor draws newcomers.

A region of cities

Found several cities side by side on a region map. Run a road across the border and people commute between them; run a power line or pipe and they trade spare power and water.

From town to metropolis

Start with streets, small zones and a coal plant. As the city grows, new eras unlock avenues and offices, then towers, hospitals and universities, then nuclear power and the airport.

Traffic that means something

Every resident commutes along your roads to a job with room for them. Busy streets jam, avenues carry more, and buses, trains and the subway take cars off the road and bring in fares.

A classic view, and a closer one

Build in a tidy isometric view with quarter turns, switch to a free 3D camera with T, or press V and walk your streets at eye level.

A city that comes alive

Day and night, lit windows, cars and people on the streets, fires to fight and milestones to celebrate as the population grows.

From source

With Rust stable:

git clone https://github.com/rsantacroce/parcels
cd parcels
cargo run --release --bin parcels

Make it look better

Everything in the world is drawn from code today. The asset list has every building, vehicle and prop the game could use as a real 3D model, with sizes and a ready-to-paste prompt for Meshy's text-to-3D. Generate one, send it back, and it can go into a future release.
